Ope! — privacy

Effective July 17, 2026

Short version: Ope! is a small cozy walking game. It does not ask for an account, does not know who you are, and does not collect personal information. Your progress is saved only on your own device.

What we collect

Anonymous visit counts

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count how many times the game is opened. It records a rough total of visits and referring pages. It sets no cookies, uses no advertising identifier (no IDFA), and does not build a profile of you or track you across other apps or websites. There is no way for us to connect a visit back to a person.

Your saved game

Your progress — the items you've collected, the neighborhoods you've discovered, your dibs — is stored locally on your device and never leaves it. We can't see it, and it isn't sent anywhere. Deleting the app (or clearing the site data) erases it.

What we do NOT collect

  • No accounts, names, emails, or passwords.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of data — there is nothing to sell.

Children

Ope! is friendly for all ages. Because it collects no personal information and has no accounts, it does not knowingly gather data from children or anyone else.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
