# Eli Bates Fountain — "Storks at Play" (reference brief)

Filed by the supervisor 2026-07-26 after the owner supplied a source link, closing
the imagery gap that task 122 flagged ("Bates fountain has a Commons gap — hand-model
from the documented type"). **The gap is now closed** — see the on-disk photo below.

## What it is

- **Sculptors:** Augustus Saint-Gaudens with his assistant Frederick William
  MacMonnies (MacMonnies later did the Grand Barge of State at the 1893 Columbian
  Exposition).
- **Installed:** 1887, a bequest of **Eli Bates** (1806–1881), Chicago lumber
  merchant — the same will that paid for Saint-Gaudens' *Standing Lincoln*.
- **Where:** dead center of the **formal garden ("The Great Garden")** on the axis
  directly **south of the Lincoln Park Conservatory** (Joseph L. Silsbee, 1890).
  41°55′22″N 87°38′7″W.
- **Name note:** the birds are commonly called storks; herons/geese are both argued.
  Build them as the documented type — long-necked wading birds, wings out.

## Composition (authoritative)

A large **circular granite basin**, and at the center:

- **Two bronze storks/herons** with **outstretched wings**, water **spouting from
  their beaks**.
- **Three half-boy / half-fish figures** (merboys), each wrestling an **unwieldy
  fish**.
- **Bronze reeds and cattails** rising at the center of the group.

## Form notes read off the photo (`Lincoln_Park_Conservatory_(9719113515).jpg`)

This is the single most useful reference on disk — it is the **lp-bates and
lp-conservatory framing in one shot**, fountain foreground, glasshouse behind.

- **The basin is BROAD and LOW.** A wide shallow grey granite ring whose rim is a
  flat sitting ledge — people are sitting and standing on it all around. It reads as
  two concentric rings: an outer rim ledge and the inner basin floor. Do not build a
  tall pedestal fountain; the mass is horizontal.
- **The center reads as a tall dark THICKET, not as figures.** From any normal
  standing distance the bronze **reeds/cattails** dominate — a spiky near-black
  vertical cluster, easily 3–4× the height of the birds. The storks and merboys read
  as **dark silhouettes at rim height around its base**, wings out. For a toon build
  at player scale this is the win condition: get the dark reed cluster + low grey
  ring right and the piece is recognizable; the figures are silhouette detail.
- **Water** is a modest low plume around the reed cluster plus the beak spouts —
  nothing tall or dramatic. Reuse the Millennium/Crown spray vocabulary.
- **Surround:** a **red-brown paved apron** rings the basin, then lawn, then
  symmetric parterre beds in hot colors (reds, magenta, silver-white edging) with a
  clipped **central lawn axis running south** away from the conservatory. Globe lamps
  on tall posts flank the axis; evergreens and small ornamental trees screen the
  glasshouse base.

## Conservatory notes from the same photo (bonus — confirms the 116 ruling)

- The **vestibule is on the SOUTH face**, facing down the garden axis — exactly the
  116 re-siting ruling, and the owner approved it 2026-07-26. It is a **low glass
  pyramid** with pale framing (silver-green glass, not opaque white), fronting the
  main mass, with a **dark green awning band over the entry doors** at its base.
- The **Palm House profile is an OGEE, not a plain arch** — the roof sweeps up in a
  soft double curve to a flat copper ridge cap, with lower glass wings stepping down
  east and west. Glass reads pale grey-green with white glazing bars; a **warm
  rusticated stone base band** runs under the glass.
- A tall modern high-rise sits behind it to the NW in real life.
