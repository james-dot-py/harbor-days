## Root cause

110 and 111 are Fable-model tasks (planner tagged `110 scout / 111 layout / 114 hero` as `model: fable`). Every spawn died at turn 1:

```
api_error_status: 429   terminal_reason: api_error   num_turns: 1
"You've reached your Fable 5 limit. Run /usage-credits to continue or switch models with /model."
rate_limit: overageStatus "rejected", overageDisabledReason "org_level_disabled",
            rateLimitType "seven_day_overage_included", resetsAt ~2026-07-24
```

(logs `session-2026-07-20T01-05-13/-19/-26/-30/-35Z`.) Six of these 1-turn crashes at 01:05 UTC burned the 3-failure park counter for **both** tasks inside one minute — they were parked back-to-back (`49fba67` 110, `c40bf8d` 111). They did not fail on merit; they never got a real session.

### Secondary bug in run.mjs (recommend hardening)

`LIMIT_RE` (run.mjs:243) does **not** match "reached your Fable 5 limit" — it only matches "usage limit / limit reached / limit exceeded / resets at / too many requests". So `looksLikeLimit()` returned false, `classifyLimit()` returned null, and the loop treated each 429 as an ordinary failure (strike + park) instead of taking its limit path (sleep + resume). This is exactly the class of billing-cap cascade the `SPEND_RE`/`AUTH_RE` guards were added to stop, just via a phrase they don't cover. Suggested fix: extend the alternation (`reached your .*limit|Fable \d+ limit|/usage-?credits|switch models`) and treat an org-overage-disabled 429 as a spend-class HALT (no strike), not a per-task failure.
